Reasons for Valuation

Dhawan Associates Property Valuer

Purpose and Reasons of Valuations

  • Valuations required for purposes ranging from open market transaction to compulsory purchase
  • Underlying method should not depend on the purpose of valuation
  • Purpose must be determined before undertaking any calculation

Need of Valuations

  • Buying and selling property involves many variables to consider
  • Without valuation, the process is unreliable and risky
  • Self-valuation is risky and almost certainly results in inaccurate value
  • Professional valuation ensures reliable investment decisions

Market Value Applications

💰

Sale Report

Estimate of price for a future date after the property has been fully marketed. A valuation for purchase is an estimate of the individual's best bid.

🏦

Loan Security

Banks commission valuation of property as collateral. They judge loan amount based on "loan to value" ratio to manage risk.

🛡

Insurance

Valuation for replacement cost in case of destruction. Based on cost of replacing the building, not including land value.

🏛

Compulsory Purchase

Public schemes involve compulsory purchase orders. Compensation based on open market value for specialised properties.

📈

Accounting Purposes

Property value reported in company accounts. A statement of the company's wealth on a particular date.

🔑

Minimum Price / Auction Reserve

Companies selling assets by tender or auction accept offers in excess of valuation. Reserve value for unusual properties.

📄

Taxation

Valuation for tax purposes. Principal taxes fall into capital and revenue groups. Often formula-based valuations.

🏠

Mortgage & Refinancing

Valuation required when refinancing a property or accessing equity. Lenders rely on accurate valuation to determine loan eligibility.
