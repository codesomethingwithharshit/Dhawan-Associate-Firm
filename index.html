Definition & Need of Valuation

  • Estimating the value of real estate is necessary for financing, sales listing, investment analysis, property insurance, and taxation
  • Valuation is needed when borrowing to buy a new property, refinancing, or accessing equity in your home
  • Based on the highest and best use of real property — what use will produce the highest possible value
  • The final appraisal must be both profitable and probable
  • Determining the asking or purchase price is the most useful application of real estate valuation

How to Select Your Property Valuer

  • Be very clear about the purpose for which valuation is required
  • The valuer should be accredited with the authorities to whom your report will be submitted
  • Take concurrence of the valuer regarding the scope of work before entrusting
  • An expert valuer with necessary background, experience and qualifications should be appointed
  • Valuation work demands professional skill — a valuer should be conscientious, highly trained, energetic and have strong integrity
  • Curiosity plus a "nose" for un-earthing hidden values is essential
  • Best policy is to entrust valuation to a professional team like Dhawan Associates Property Valuer
  • Professional firms ensure (a) transparency (b) fairness at all stages (c) reasonable charges
