Property Valuation

Dhawan Associates – IBBI Registered Property Valuer

What is a Property Valuation

  • A report outlining property assets and elements affecting value
  • Includes square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, backyard amenities & location proximity
  • Valuation determines the amount for which property will transact on a particular date
  • Specialised properties require valuation models based on wealth-producing qualities
  • Valuer inspects interior, exterior & surrounding neighbourhood objectively
  • Factors considered: living space, construction quality, location, layout, lot size, condition, renovations
  • Living space excludes garage, porches, sheds; basements calculated separately

Price, Worth and Market Value

  • Property valuation identifies assets and value-affecting elements of a property
  • Land value depends on demand & supply for the product produced
  • Valuation is the determination of transaction amount on a specific date
  • Real estate valuation is central to all businesses using land as a factor of production

Different Methods of Property Valuation

🏠

Land and Building Method

Land: Circle rates, bonafied transactions, demand & locality, opinions of relevant persons.

Building: Plinth area rates based on CPWD/State PWD, adjusted by Index cost, Present Value.

💶

Rental Capitalization Method

Capitalizing net annual Rental Income at appropriate rate. Net Rent = Gross Rental Income – outgoings (Property tax, repairs, maintenance, insurance, etc.) approx. 40%.

🏗

Development Method

For properties with developmental potential. Large land divided into plots, or multi-storied complexes. Cost decided per acre (4840 Sqyd).

📊

Profit Method

Applicable to Hotels, Cinemas, PVRS, Marriage Halls. Based on working profit from property then capitalizing at appropriate rate. Considers AV of Net Profit & Goodwill.

Valuation Considerations

  • Guide lines from Registrar's Department (Circle rates)
  • Price paid within a reasonable time, in bonafied transactions of purchase of lands acquired
  • Demand, locality, characteristics like shape, size and location of Roads and Parks
  • Opinions of relevant person such as Neighbors, Brokers and recent sales and prevailing trend
  • Plinth area rates bases on CPWD or State PWD and adjusted by Index cost
  • Present Value of Building
